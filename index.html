The app

More context.
Less guesswork.

Built for a personal League of Legends workflow. The app runs locally, with statistics collected through the Riot API.

Draft context

Review your assigned role, team picks, and bans. Estimated enemy roles retain uncertainty for flex picks.

Patch-specific statistics

Compare champions by role using observed and smoothed win rates. Samples stay separate by patch, region, rank, and queue.

A pool that fits you

Maintain your own champion pool and optionally use it to narrow recommendations. Inspect the evidence behind each suggestion.

Local data collection

Compare eligible ranked games from the last 14 days, with samples separated by patch, region, rank, and queue.

Data & privacy

Your draft
stays on your device.

Draft state, settings, and aggregate match statistics are processed locally. Your own Riot API key is used for direct Riot API requests.

This website does not request an API key, connect to your League client, or distribute the desktop app.

Read the privacy information

Statistics with visible limitsResults come from a limited player sample, not every game worldwide. Scores are comparisons, not win probabilities. No result is a promise of winning.

The project is in private development. A public information page and verification file do not represent Riot approval or a public release.
